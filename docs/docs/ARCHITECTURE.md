### Mission
Identify and analyze the convergence between an individual's skills and real market demand, transforming this convergence into a testable and potentially monetizable economic opportunity.

### Core Principles
- Evidence over speculation
- Demand over assumption
- Problems before ideas
- Market reality before business projections
- No invented statistics
- Clear distinction between facts, data, signals, hypotheses and interpretations

### Application Objectives
- Interactive step-by-step analysis
- Malagasy, French and English languages
- User accounts
- Saved analyses
- AI-assisted market analysis
- Business opportunity reports
- Android-compatible interface

### Development Principle
DNA-CORE must be a genuine application, not merely a chatbot or a static form.
